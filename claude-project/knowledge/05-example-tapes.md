Myself and my partner spoke with **Ian Hideous** from **Black Country Extra** radio station for an interview regarding the high strangeness that happened in our home, which is fully documented in *Report 001: The Stranger at the Door*.

In this unnerving account, we delve into a firsthand experience that started as unexpected knocking in the dead of night. What begins as a dismissible event quickly spirals into an eerie sequence of activity that left our household shaken to its core.
