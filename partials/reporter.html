Matt

Matt has been encountering the unexplained since he was a child. In the summer of 2020, a series of events began at his family home, and that case became Incident Report 001: The Stranger at the Door.

Since then, he has been collecting first-hand accounts from family, friends and colleagues, and filing them as Incident Reports. He looks at every case through a logical lens first, then lets the witnesses speak for themselves.

If something has happened to you that you can’t explain, submit an incident. Your account could be the next report we file!
