Report Introduction

This report comes from a family member. I remember how it all started quite clearly, even though it was a long time ago now. I was around 13 or 14 (at the time of writing, I’m 35), sitting in the living room at my Nan’s house in Smethwick, Birmingham, watching an episode of Most Haunted. In my opinion it was a particularly good one, and it did the rounds when I was a kid: the episode with the famous barrel that seemed to roll itself down a cellar corridor (Season 4, Episode 12: The Greengate Brewery in Middleton). I have no idea whether it was faked, but I remember feeling like my teenage mind was being blown. To me at the time, it was yet more proof that the paranormal was real.

I was in the living room with a close family member who grew up in that house. She has asked to remain anonymous, which I completely understand, so for the sake of this account we’ll call her Fran. From my own relationship with Fran, I can say that she has no interest in the paranormal or the supernatural whatsoever. Growing up, even trying to have a conversation with her about it was hard work, because she was so dismissive of the whole subject.

Back to Most Haunted. I was watching in absolute amazement as the beer barrel rolled down a dark, dusty corridor in grainy night vision, when Fran suddenly scoffed behind me. I turned around, half amused that she was even paying attention, because up to that point she’d shown no interest in the show at all. I remember our conversation going something along the following lines:

“You think that’s scary?” she said. “I’ve had way scarier things happen to me, and I think this is a load of rubbish.”

I took the bait. “Yeah, if you say so. Nothing’s ever happened to you. I’ve asked you a million times and you’ve always told me to stop wasting your time.”

Her reply came in a far more serious tone than I was expecting. “No, I really have. It happened here, in your Nan’s house, when I was a little girl.”

In that moment, I was more blown away by Fran admitting to an experience than by anything happening on the TV.

“What happened?” I asked, amazed that this incredibly rare window had opened.

For context, my grandparents were very strict when Fran lived in the house, and getting out of bed at night would land you in a lot of trouble. Fran explained that when she was a little girl, she would often hear children playing on the landing outside her door after she’d gone to bed. On one occasion, one of the children came into her bedroom and asked her to come out and play. Fran panicked and told the child she couldn’t, because she’d get into trouble for getting out of bed. She was also frightened that her parents would hear them talking and wake up, which would get her into even more trouble. In the end, she asked the child to leave her bedroom and not come back. The child left, but Fran could still hear them playing on the stairs for some time afterwards.

I couldn’t believe it. Someone I had known for years was telling me about a full, authentic experience from her past. I started firing questions at her, desperate for more detail, but she shut me down and slipped straight back into her usual “this is all a load of rubbish” mode. For years afterwards I tried to get more out of her, but I never managed it.

That changed recently, when a chance conversation led to Fran finally agreeing to let me interview her properly about what happened. The account below is told by Fran herself, and it differs greatly from the version she told me as a teenager. Part of me thinks she deliberately downplayed it back then so as not to scare me. Fran still does not believe in the supernatural, and that is exactly why I think this account is so rare: it’s a genuinely frightening experience, told by a sceptic. Before we delve into Fran’s story, a little history on the location:

The House

Our story takes place in a council house on Astbury Avenue in Smethwick. I spent a lot of my childhood at this house, and I only have great memories of it. My sister feels the same. It was a place we loved going to, and my grandparents were, and still are, incredible people.

Fran’s memories of the house are different, and so are those of another family member, who I’ll also keep anonymous. For the sake of this report, we’ll call him John. This was a time during Fran and John’s childhood when many working-class families felt the effects of alcohol abuse and everything that comes with it, and Astbury Avenue was no exception. I’ll leave that part of the house’s history there. It isn’t a memory I share, and it isn’t my story to speculate on. The reason I included this bit of information is to show the potential energy in the house during their childhood.

I’ve done some research into the house, and from what I understand, an old lady lived there before my Nan and Grandad. They originally lodged with her, and I remember my Nan telling me that she cared for the old lady when she became poorly towards the end of her life, right up until she passed away (in the house, as far as I can recall). After that, my grandparents stopped being lodgers and took over the property permanently.

From my research, the house appears to have been built between the First and Second World Wars, when Smethwick’s council was buying up farmland on the west side of the town for new housing estates.

Before that, nothing stood on the land. So while the house is pre-war, it doesn’t carry the hundreds of years of history that many allegedly haunted properties do.

The layout of the house was simple. You’d walk through the front door into a small hallway with the stairs straight ahead and a door to the left. That door led into the living room, and another door from there led into the kitchen. The kitchen had a large walk-in pantry on the right, tucked into the space under the stairs, a downstairs toilet, and a short corridor leading out to a large back garden.

At the top of the stairs, the bathroom was directly in front of you. To the left of the bathroom, the landing carried on, with two normal-sized bedrooms off it. At the opposite end of the landing, on the other side of the bathroom, was the third bedroom: a small box room. This is where the report below takes place.

It’s important to note that Fran, John, my sister and I all suffered terrible, recurring nightmares whenever we slept in the box room. No other bedroom in the house had this effect. The other common denominator is that all four of us were children at the time we experienced the nightmares. Fran recalls that when John slept in there, his sleep was so disturbed that he would rock violently enough to bang his head against the wall repeatedly, waking my grandparents. After interviewing Fran, I spoke to John about this myself. He told me it only ever happened in the box room, never in any other bedroom in the house, and it has never happened to him since leaving the property.

What follows is told from Fran’s perspective. It’s a truly terrifying account, and I didn’t expect it to feel as sinister as it did. I was excited to hear the details of the story she’d originally told me, but what I documented made me feel extremely creeped out.

Fran’s Story

Part 1: The Box Room

I was around five or six years old and living at the house on Astbury Avenue. Apart from this one incident, I can’t recall anything paranormal ever happening to me in that house. I’ve had a few odd things happen over my lifetime, but nothing that has ever made me a believer in the supernatural. As it stands, I don’t believe in this sort of thing, and I have no interest in it beyond recording what happened to me regarding this particular event.

I was sleeping in the box room when it happened. Whenever I slept in that room, I had horrible, recurring nightmares and night terrors. Two of them I can still remember clearly.

In the first, I was running up a hill towards the road our house was on, being chased by something. I had no idea what it was. I just remember being absolutely terrified and knowing I had to reach the top of the hill. Every time I got there, I could feel this thing right behind me, and the moment I turned around to look at it, I would wake up.

The second nightmare was different. I was suspended in the air above my bed, sitting upright with my legs out in front of me. I couldn’t get myself back down onto the bed, and I couldn’t scream or call for help. The room was always dark around me. I had that dream a lot as a child when I stayed in the box room.

Part 2: The Visitor

On the night it happened, I was asleep in bed. I remember opening my eyes and realising something was at the end of my bed, tugging at the bottom of my quilt. It wasn’t trying to pull the quilt off me; it was tugging to get my attention. As my eyes adjusted to the darkness, I slowly began to make sense of what I was looking at.

There was no light in the bedroom. My bed faced out towards the landing, and at the far end of the landing was the bathroom. My bedroom door was open, the bathroom door was closed and the house was silent. It must have been the early hours of the morning, because everyone was asleep. There was a window to my right, but there wasn’t any natural light coming through it. Opposite the window was a big wardrobe built into the space over the stairs, with a large door on it.

I did see some sort of light on the landing, but it’s really hard to describe. There was a window above the stairs, so it could have been natural light coming from somewhere, but it looked more like a lamp had been switched on. There were no lamps on the landing, though. There was nothing electrical up there at all other than the landing light on the ceiling. Whatever it was, the light was bright enough to show me what was standing at the bottom of my bed.

It was a small figure, no taller than an eight-year-old child. It was completely black, like a shadow, with no facial features and nothing to tell me whether it was a boy or a girl. Even though I couldn’t see a face, I had the feeling it was a child. When it spoke to me, it felt like it spoke inside my head rather than out loud, and I can’t remember whether the voice sounded male or female.

It said, “Come and play with us.”

I told it I couldn’t, because I’d get into trouble if I woke my parents up. I spoke to it out loud as quietly as I could, but whenever it replied, it always seemed to be speaking inside my head.

“Come on,” it said. “Come with me and come and play with us downstairs.”

By now I could hear the sound of children playing downstairs. It sounded like they were in the living room. I told the figure again that I wasn’t allowed and that I’d get into trouble if I got out of bed. It was very persistent. It wanted me to come downstairs and play with it and its friends, but I kept saying no. I remember feeling as though it really wanted me to go with it. I wasn’t scared, and the figure never came across as threatening.

It asked me one more time, and again I said no. This time, the figure turned away from my bed and walked along the landing to the top of the stairs. I watched it go, and I could see it moving, so I think it had arms and legs and was walking. When it reached the stairs, it went down them, and the light on the landing went down with it. As the light faded, the sound of the children playing stopped and everything went dark. I think I fell back asleep after that.

As far as I can remember, that was the only time it happened. I have a very distant feeling that the figure may have come back and asked me to play again on another night, but I honestly can’t remember it properly, so I’d say with confidence that it only happened once.

Report Follow-Up

After interviewing Fran, I asked John whether anything like this had ever happened to him (he lived in the house with Fran when they were both children). He confirmed it hadn’t. I also remember asking my Nan and Grandad, after Fran first told me the story in their living room, whether they’d ever experienced anything like it. They both said no, and that they’d never had any paranormal experiences in the house at all. They also said they didn’t remember Fran ever telling them this story, and John doesn’t remember Fran telling him either. It appears she kept it to herself.

Addendum: Lights in the Sky

There are two more memories I think are worth including in this report. They didn’t take place in the box room, and they’re both from my childhood. I’ll explain why I’ve included them at the end.

When I was around 9 or 10 (the year would have been around 2001), my Nan would ring our family home most nights to tell my mum about something she kept seeing in the sky over the house. This probably went on for a few months. She described it as diamond-shaped and flashing beautiful colours, and she couldn’t believe what she was seeing. My mum said it was probably a star, but given my curiosity about this sort of thing, I was hooked.

One night, my Grandad actually filmed it on an old VHS camcorder. When he zoomed in on the object, it went further out of focus, and when he zoomed back out, it snapped back to normal.

I told everyone in my science class about it at school, and of course nobody believed me. So, knowing I had some hard evidence to bring to the table, I brought my Grandad’s tape into school, and the teacher wheeled out the old box TV on its trolley so we could all watch it. I’d already told the class what my Nan had described: a diamond flashing all different colours. But on the tape, the object looked exactly like the moon, nothing like what my Nan and Grandad had seen with their own eyes. The class started laughing, saying “look, it’s the moon, he was lying”, but I knew what was coming. The moon object suddenly split into two separate objects, and they shot off in different directions. The whole class went silent.

I later lent that tape to a friend and never got it back. I’m fairly sure he taped over it. I’m gutted, because it would have been amazing to include here, but I believe that footage is lost to history.

Then there was one night when my Nan was staying with us at our family home in Bromsgrove while my parents were out for the evening. Again, I would have been around the age of 10, and it was during the period when my Nan was ringing the family house about the diamond lights in the sky. We were in the back room, looking out over the garden at the stars. It was a clear night, and my Nan was telling me about the objects she and my Grandad kept seeing. I asked her whether the flashing stars could be what she’d been seeing (stars that seem to flash different colours when you look at them), and she said no, they looked nothing like them.

As we were talking, something huge flew over the house. It was almost invisible, but it had small lights along it that gave it an outline, and I can’t remember it making any sound. In terms of shape, it was like a big, rectangular craft with rounded edges. It passed over the garden and the houses behind it, made a quick U-turn, and then shot up into the night sky, and the higher it got, the more it looked like an extremely slow-moving shooting star with a huge tail behind it. My Nan and I went out the front of the house and watched it climb higher and higher until it seemed to stop moving altogether. We then came back into the house.

I have no idea what it was. Even compared with UAP accounts I’ve read about since then, I’ve never come across anything that moved quite like that. The closest thing I could describe it as being is an illuminated rectangular comet that flew over the house behind our garden and then shot back off into space, producing a tail the higher it got in the sky. Whatever it was, it absolutely amazed both of us, and it’s an experience my Nan and I shared and have never been able to explain.

Curious whether anyone else had reported anything similar, I went through the Ministry of Defence’s published UFO report summaries for 1999 to 2004. There’s nothing logged over Smethwick itself, and no record of a diamond-shaped light seen night after night, which isn’t surprising given my Nan only ever rang my mum about it and never reported it officially.

There are, however, a handful of reports from around the region during the same period:

  • December 2000, Rugeley: An object was seen hovering above a tree line, changing from white to blue to red and back to white again.
  • 11 August 2001, Tipton: Only a few miles from Smethwick, a bright round light that moved in steps, shrank, and then shot off to the left and disappeared.
  • March 2001, Moseley: A very bright light, followed a couple of hours later by a dark object with lights underneath it.
  • March 2002, Leicester: A light larger than a star that turned and appeared diamond-shaped.

Closer to the garden sighting that my Nan and I experienced, a few strange reports came in from around the Worcester area (Bromsgrove is within the council district of Worcestershire):

  • December 2001, Malvern: A star-shaped object with red lights in a triangle and yellow and blue rays. It hovered, pulsated, then vanished.
  • 31 May 2001, Worcester: A very bright shooting star that moved slowly and then very fast.
  • 15 April 2002, Worcester: A big, round, bright star-like object, coloured yellow and red, which moved upwards (evening).
  • 29 August 2002, Leicester: It looked like a shooting star, with four lights on each side, like a black shadow.

These are the closest officially documented reports I’ve ever come across to what my Nan and I experienced. Worcester and Malvern are both very local to Bromsgrove. The Leicester sighting isn’t close to where we had our experience, but it’s a great short description of the type of craft we saw, which was only visible due to the lights making its outline visible against the night sky before creating the shooting star tail effect.

None of this proves what my Nan, my Grandad or I saw, and a star-like light flashing different colours is one of the most common descriptions in the MoD files, often down to bright stars twinkling low in the sky. However, it does show that similar things were being reported across the Midlands at around the same time, and it’s the first time I’ve actually felt some closure reading reports of something that is similar to what my Nan and I experienced.

My Thoughts

I included the memories above for a reason. Personally, I don’t think what Fran encountered was paranormal in the traditional sense. It sounds much closer to the UAP and NHI (non-human intelligence) accounts I’ve come across in my research and in the books I’ve read. As stated previously, when I was a small child I remember my Nan ringing us because of the diamond lights, and we had that shared experience at my house in Bromsgrove. Could the lights in the sky my Nan reported seeing, the back garden experience we shared and what happened to Fran be connected in some way? I think that’s a massive stretch personally, but I thought it was worth including all the same.

I also wanted to rule out sleep paralysis with Fran’s report, so I asked her whether she was paralysed when she woke up. As far as I know, she has never experienced sleep paralysis. I have experienced it many times in my life, and I believe I have a good understanding of how to identify it. I explained what it is, and she gave me a firm no. She remembers waking up and pushing herself upright as her eyes adjusted to the dark, and she could move and talk freely.

The more sceptical among us will point out that ruling out sleep paralysis doesn’t rule out a hypnopompic hallucination, a vivid, dream-like experience in the moments as you wake up, which can include seeing a figure by the bed or feeling the covers being tugged. In my opinion, it’s the most obvious explanation, and I’ve thought about it a lot. I’ve experienced this myself, too.

What makes me hesitate is how much of Fran’s account doesn’t fit neatly. She sat up and moved freely, she held a back-and-forth conversation with the figure rather than simply seeing it, and the figure, the light and the sound of children playing all left together, fading down the stairs at the same moment. From what Fran said, it also sounded like this experience lasted for a while. None of that proves anything, but it’s why I don’t think this can be written off quite as easily as Fran would like.

Fran also had recurring nightmares of being suspended above her bed, and then a conversation with a small, featureless figure that seemed to speak to her telepathically. Both of those details echo a lot of similar accounts from other incidents that involve UAP and NHI.

Once I’d spoken to Fran, it got me thinking: what would have happened if she had got out of bed and followed that figure down the stairs? Interestingly, both folklore and UFO lore have surprisingly similar answers.

In British fae tradition, children were said to be lured away by the fair folk, often with an invitation to play or the sound of music and revelry coming from somewhere just out of sight. Those who went rarely came back unchanged. Some were never seen again, some returned to find years had passed in what felt like a single night, and anyone who ate or drank in the fae realm was said to be trapped there for good. Many of these stories describe the fair folk relying on a child coming willingly rather than taking them by force, which makes me wonder whether Fran’s repeated “no” was the very thing that sent the figure back down the stairs alone.

UFO researchers have noticed the same patterns in modern abduction accounts, most famously Jacques Vallée in his 1969 book Passport to Magonia, which lays out the parallels between fae abductions and alien encounters: small beings, paralysis, strange lights, missing time, and people being floated from their beds. Seen through that lens, Fran’s recurring dream of floating above her bed, and a small featureless figure speaking directly into her head, could easily sit alongside an abduction account, one in which going downstairs might have meant waking up back in bed hours later with little or no memory of where she’d been.
